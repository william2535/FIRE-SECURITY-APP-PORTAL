Practise Protec 6500 loop diagnostics

  • Compare Map groups with a supplied fictional survey, and learn why tied or missing Map values cannot establish exact cable order.
  • Work through three virtual cases: a mapped loop, intermittent replies and an incomplete map.
  • Compare repeated one-hour simulated counter readings and polling coverage, so fewer reported losses are not mistaken for a healthy loop.
  • Use step-by-step hints, checked menu references and citations to the Protec commissioning manual.
  • Resume a saved exercise, export its evidence log, and record uncertainties and restoration checks before finishing.

Training simulation only, not a full Protec 6500 emulator. It does not connect to or program a live panel. Manual references identify the applicable edition; this update does not announce a new APK or Play Store release.

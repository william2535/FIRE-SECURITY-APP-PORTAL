Built by an engineer.
For engineers.

I started building these apps during my fire and security apprenticeship while working full time. I develop them on my own, in my spare time.

My goal is simple: better, affordable tools for the fire and security community.

Free browser builds. No subscriptions. No sign-in needed. The core apps are designed around local storage: your jobs, drawings and downloaded manuals stay on your device, with no built-in advertising trackers or developer analytics collecting your work. Website hosts may still process basic connection data.

Optional AI in FINDR. Download a local AI model to ask questions about panel manuals and documents you have saved, without sending those questions to an online AI service. FINDR also offers separately selected online AI, which sends requests to the chosen provider.

Built from real on-site experience and improved through community feedback.

03 / INSTALL THE APPS

Choose how to install.

GET IT ONGoogle Play⌄

Help test Zone Sketch, FINDR and Jobs on your Android phone or tablet before their public release.

01

Sign in and request to join

Sign in to Google Groups using the Google account you'll use on Google Play. Open the testing group and request to join.

JOIN GOOGLE GROUP ↗
02

Wait for approval

You must wait for me to accept your Google Group request. You won't be able to access the closed tests until your account has been approved and given access.

03

Become a tester

Once approved, open the testing link for each app you want to try and press Become a tester.

Zone SketchJOIN TEST ↗ FINDRJOIN TEST ↗ JobsJOIN TEST ↗
04

Install through Google Play

After opting in, follow the Google Play link on the testing page to install the app. Use the same Google account you joined the group with.

05

Stay opted in for 14 days

Google requires at least 12 testers per app for 14 days for this closed-testing stage. Please stay opted in, try the apps when you can, and share any bugs or suggestions.

Want to test the paid apps?

Zone Sketch (£6.99) and Jobs (£5.99) have promotional codes available for testers when active. Contact me for a code if needed. FINDR is free.

♟FREE BROWSER BUILDAndroid shortcut⌄

Save any free browser build to your Android home screen for quick access.

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu ⋮.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm to add its icon to your home screen.

Choose a free browser build from the app cards above.

FREE BROWSER BUILDApple web app⌄

Save a free browser build to your iPhone or iPad home screen.

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App if offered, then tap Add.

The app will appear on your home screen. Offline features depend on the individual app and what you have saved.

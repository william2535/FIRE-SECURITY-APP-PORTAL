03 / GOOGLE PLAY CLOSED TESTING

How to install the apps.

Help test Zone Sketch, FINDR and Jobs on your Android phone or tablet before their public release.

01

Sign in and request to join

Sign in to Google Groups using the Google account you'll use on Google Play. Open the testing group and request to join.

JOIN GOOGLE GROUP ↗
02

Wait for approval

You must wait for me to accept your Google Group request. You won't be able to access the closed tests until your account has been approved and given access.

03

Become a tester

Once approved, open the testing link for each app you want to try and press Become a tester.

Zone SketchJOIN TEST ↗ FINDRJOIN TEST ↗ JobsJOIN TEST ↗
04

Install through Google Play

After opting in, follow the Google Play link on the testing page to install the app. Use the same Google account you joined the group with.

05

Stay opted in for 14 days

Google requires at least 12 testers per app for 14 days for this closed-testing stage. Please stay opted in, try the apps when you can, and share any bugs or suggestions.

Want to test the paid apps?

Zone Sketch (£6.99) and Jobs (£5.99) have promotional codes available for testers when active. Contact me for a code if needed. FINDR is free.
